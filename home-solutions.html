Interior & Modular Solutions

The kitchen and storage spaces in a home carry most of its daily wear, so we design modular kitchens and custom wardrobes around how you actually use the space, not just how it looks on day one. False ceilings conceal wiring and lighting runs while adding depth to a room, and our space optimisation work makes the most of awkward layouts (box rooms, narrow hallways, low-light corners) rather than working around them. Where a home needs more than one room refreshed, we take on full interior renovations from design through to handover.

  • Modular kitchens
  • Custom wardrobes
  • False ceiling designs
  • Space optimisation
  • Interior renovations

Smart Home Solutions

A smart home should make daily life simpler, not add another set of apps to manage. We install home automation that brings lighting, climate and appliances onto one reliable control point, alongside smart security built around CCTV and video door phones, so you always know who’s at the door, whether you’re home or away. IoT-based controls tie these systems together, letting you monitor and adjust your home remotely instead of juggling separate gadgets.

  • Home automation systems
  • Smart security solutions
  • CCTV installation
  • Video door phones
  • IoT-based home controls

Energy-Efficient Living

Lower energy bills start with understanding where the energy is going. Many engagements begin with a home energy audit that finds where a property is losing efficiency before we recommend fixes. From there, residential solar can offset a significant share of a household’s grid consumption, and EV charger installation prepares a home for an electric vehicle without an awkward retrofit later. Throughout, we look at the property as a whole system rather than upgrading one appliance at a time.

  • Residential solar
  • EV charger installation
  • Home energy optimisation
  • Energy audits
