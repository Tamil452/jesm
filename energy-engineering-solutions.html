Renewable Energy Solutions

Every solar or wind project carries risk that’s easier to manage before construction than after it. Our team supports solar plant engineering and wind energy consulting from early concept through to execution, running feasibility studies that test a site’s technical and commercial viability before capital is committed. Where a project connects to the existing grid, we handle grid integration and the technical studies utilities require for approval. For investors and lenders, we carry out independent technical due diligence, giving a clear view of a project’s engineering risk ahead of financial close.

  • Solar plant engineering
  • Wind energy consulting
  • Feasibility studies
  • Grid integration support
  • Technical due diligence

Project Engineering Support

Power system studies are where designs meet real-world electrical behaviour. We run load flow analysis to confirm a network can carry its intended load, short circuit studies to size equipment correctly, and arc flash studies to set the safety boundaries your maintenance teams work within. Protection coordination studies make sure that when a fault occurs, the right breaker trips first, isolating the problem without taking down more of the network than necessary. We model in both ETAP and DIgSILENT PowerFactory, so the output fits whichever standard your organisation or regulator already uses.

  • Power system studies
  • Load flow analysis
  • Short circuit studies
  • Arc flash studies
  • Protection coordination studies
  • ETAP studies
  • DIgSILENT PowerFactory studies

Substation Engineering

A substation sits where reliability matters most, and small design errors there carry outsized consequences. We provide AIS and GIS substation design, covering primary engineering (switchgear, transformers and busbar layout) alongside secondary engineering for the control, protection and metering systems that keep it running safely. Our protection and control work spans EHV and HV substations, giving utilities and industrial clients one engineering partner across the full voltage range they operate.

  • AIS/GIS substation design
  • Primary engineering
  • Secondary engineering
  • Protection & control design
  • EHV/HV substation solutions

Engineering Resource Solutions

Not every project needs a full consulting engagement. Sometimes what’s missing is capacity. We provide contract engineering support and design engineers who embed directly into your project team, scaling up during peak phases without the overhead of permanent hires. For harder-to-fill roles, we draw on specialist consultants and run technical recruitment for engineering and power-sector positions, so staffing gaps get closed by people who already understand the domain.

  • Contract engineering support
  • Design engineering resources
  • Project staffing
  • Specialist consultants
  • Technical recruitment
